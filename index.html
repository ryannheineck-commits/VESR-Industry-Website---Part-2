Core Values

About these values

Here are my four main values. For each one I say what it means, where it comes from, why it matters, and what might happen if I do not stick to it.

Ambition
  1. What it means I believe you must believe in yourself and set goals to make them happen.
  2. Where it comes from Seeing people I admire aim high and reach their goals showed me how far ambition can take you.
  3. Why it matters Ambition gives me focus and the push to keep going when things get hard.
  4. Ramifications Without ambition you might drift and miss chances to grow and succeed.
Experience
  1. What it means I think the more time and effort you put into something the more you get out of it.
  2. Where it comes from Doing long projects and practice taught me that steady work builds skill.
  3. Why it matters Experience helps me make better choices and do work I can be proud of.
  4. Ramifications Skipping the work leads to shallow results and repeated mistakes.
Independence
  1. What it means I want to be able to take care of myself and not rely on others to get by.
  2. Where it comes from Watching family and learning to solve problems on my own showed me why independence matters.
  3. Why it matters Being independent lets me make my own choices and handle changes with more confidence.
  4. Ramifications Depending on others too much can limit freedom and slow down personal growth.
Originality
  1. What it means I value doing what I want with my life and not listening to people who are negative.
  2. Where it comes from Creative projects and advice to follow my own path taught me to trust my ideas.
  3. Why it matters Original choices keep my work real and keep me excited to learn and make things.
  4. Ramifications If you follow only what others say you can lose passion and regret missed chances.

Short reflection

I work on these values by setting goals, practicing regularly, solving problems on my own and following projects that feel right for me.
